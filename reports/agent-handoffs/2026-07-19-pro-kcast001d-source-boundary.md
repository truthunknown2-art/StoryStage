## Lower-face replacement-patch ruling

Pro accepted a full nose, muzzle, and mouth replacement patch for Ollo under hard machine-enforced conditions:

- `mouth-rest` declares `replacementMode: "exclusive"`;
- `ownedFeatures` are exactly `nose`, `muzzle`, and `mouth`;
- all eight rest/viseme patches share one `registrationGroup`, output canvas, pivot, nose anchor, and z-order;
- every patch has an identical muzzle outer silhouette and alpha boundary;
- nose, muzzle, shading, and texture pixels outside one declared `mouthChangeBounds` are byte-identical;
- only the mouth, its interior, and immediately affected lip pixels may differ inside that rectangle;
- the head source contains no second nose, muzzle, or mouth underneath;
- the renderer swaps the entire lower-face patch atomically;
- pairwise pixel delta outside `mouthChangeBounds` is exactly zero after deterministic normalization.

The diagnostic must also include a rapid `rest → AI → rest → MBP → rest → OH` exposure sequence at two or three frames per exposure. Any nose or muzzle shimmer rejects the source.

## Paw identity ruling

Five-lobed or human five-finger hands are rejected before intake. Ollo requires separately authored left and right four-digit mitten paws: three rounded fingers plus one thumb, clear wrist attachment, no touching forearm pixels, no clipped lobes, no automatic mirroring, and no realistic human knuckles or narrow fingers.
