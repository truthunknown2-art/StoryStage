## Finished-MP4 pass rubric

Every item is pass/fail and must be judged on the final verified delivery, not the editor preview.

- **Story clarity:** without reading the script, a reviewer can identify the lone dancer, escalation to public emergency, official music/stage response, exhaustion theory, and budget punchline.
- **Editorial variety:** the 26.33-second episode visibly uses environment, presenter performance, reconstruction, diagram, kinetic type, and reaction. No visual idea remains materially unchanged for more than four seconds.
- **Pacing:** shots land on narration clause boundaries; every post-establishing cut is intentional; no camera move fights a text reveal.
- **Presenter use:** Rook appears as an anchor and punchline device, not as a full-episode talking wallpaper. Pose swaps and framing changes read at normal playback speed.
- **Reconstruction honesty:** every generated historical scene carries a legible `Generated reconstruction` disclosure throughout its appearance and is listed with cleared generated-media provenance in delivery evidence.
- **Information graphics:** both diagrams communicate their causal sequence in under one second at 1080p and remain readable on a laptop-sized player.
- **Typography:** emphasized words are readable in under one second, safe from caption collisions, and do more than repeat a lower-third subtitle.
- **Composition:** no accidental crop, empty placeholder, matte fringe, stretched image, UI watermark, or production-debug label appears in the verified master.
- **Audio:** narration is intelligible over the reviewed music/SFX decision; cuts and semantic accents support rather than obscure spoken words; no unapproved media is audible.
- **Delivery integrity:** two renders of the exact saved revision pass deterministic comparison, the published delivery reopens after restart, captions match the approved timing, and every consumed medium has content-bound rights evidence.

The pilot is publishable only when all ten rubric items pass and StoryStage reopens a current verified delivery bundle for the exact approved production revision.
